Privacy Policy

Swalath — Quran, Swalaths, Duas and Prayer Times

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Swalath app handles information when you use the app.

Location Permission

If you grant location permission, the app may use your device location to provide location-related features such as local prayer times. You can deny or revoke this permission in your device settings. Some features may not work without it. We do not use location permission to track your movements.

Notifications and Alarms

Notifications and alarm-related permissions may be used to provide prayer-time reminders and Adhan features.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store preferences locally on your device, including your selected language, theme, favourites and counters. You can remove locally stored app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

Some features, including online Quran audio or other online content, may connect to third-party services. These services may receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your device connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Personal Information

We do not sell personal information. The app's information handling depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to us.

Data Security

We take a careful approach to information handling, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions and notifications through your device settings. Disabling certain permissions may affect related features.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
